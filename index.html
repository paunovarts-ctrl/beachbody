Your zones

Zones group the beach into areas. Beds and parasols are assigned to a zone automatically, by where they sit on the map. Use Draw zone on the beach map to trace a new shape point by point.

How the spot markers work

Sun beds and parasols are interchangeable — it’s the spot on the concrete that’s fixed, not the furniture. Print a marker for each spot and fix it to the ground — a painted number, a floor decal, or a small screwed-down plate. At night, stack and lock the furniture as always; each morning any bed or parasol can go on any open marker of its kind — nothing to match by QR, nothing to reorganise.

Don’t stick markers on the furniture itself — once beds are piled and locked up, a code on the bed tells you nothing about where it goes back.

Actions

Signing in

Operator Admin1, passcode Admin. The passcode hint is printed under the form. Sign out with the arrow at the bottom of the left rail.

The left rail

Five icons, top to bottom: Overview, Beach map, Zones & setup, Day & takings and Scan. Hover any icon to see its name.

Devices & clock

Top right shows how many devices have this beach open right now, plus the time. A Saved badge blinks whenever a change is written to storage.

Sound

The speaker at the bottom of the rail mutes every sound effect. Check-in, set free, hold and service each have their own tone so you can work without looking.

Language

The EN / HR button switches the whole app between English and Croatian, including dates and weekday names. Your choice is remembered.

Fullscreen

Expands the app to the whole screen — handy on a counter tablet. Press F or click the button again to leave.

Beach Overview card

A live miniature of the beach with every bed as a coloured dot. Click anywhere on it to slide straight into the full map.

The four callouts

Free now and In use count everything together. Sun beds and Parasols show free out of total for each item type, since they are charged separately.

Peak occupancy

One bar per closed day — the busiest moment of that day as a share of the whole beach. Hover a bar for the exact date, occupancy, check-ins and takings.

Takings

Today is live, This week adds the last seven closed days, Best day is your record. The green pill shows how much of the beach is in use right now.

Check-ins through the week

How many guests were checked in each day. The dot marks today, which is still counting.

Zones list

Every zone with its bed and parasol count and how many are free. Click a row to jump to the map and fly to that zone.

Scanner shortcut

The card at the bottom of the left column opens the camera scanner in one click.

Moving around

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use + / − at the bottom left. The satellite photo is your real beach, so positions match what you see outside.

Zone chips

The row above the map filters and flies to a zone. All beach zooms back out to everything.

Reading the colours

Green free, red in use, blue held, grey out of service. A small dot on the corner repeats the status as a shape, so it still reads if colours are hard to tell apart.

Tapping a bed

Opens a card beside it with status, guest and time. The four buttons set Free, Hold, Check in or Service. Type a guest name in the field, or use Scan and Remove at the bottom.

Map buttons

Down the right edge: pencil opens arrange mode, then whole beach to fit everything on screen, scan for the camera, and reset demo to restore the sample layout.

Arrange tools

The pencil reveals a rail on the left. Select drags beds, Place one drops a single item, Place a row takes a start and end click, Place a set drops two beds with a parasol behind them.

Beds or parasols

The two item buttons decide what the placing tools drop. Parasols are a separate charged item with their own price and their own count.

Row length

The small number box sets how many items a row gets. Anything from 2 to 40.

Snap and undo

Snap to grid lines new items up neatly. Undo steps back through placements — or press Ctrl+Z. The arrow at the top collapses the whole rail.

Keyboard shortcuts

E arrange mode · Ctrl+Z undo · Delete removes the selected bed · Esc closes a card, the scanner or cancels drawing · Enter closes a zone shape · F fullscreen.

What zones are for

Zones split the beach into named areas. Beds join a zone automatically by where they sit, so you never assign them by hand.

Drawing a zone

Draw a new zone jumps to the map with the pen armed. Click point by point around the area, then click the first point again or press Enter to close it. Esc cancels.

Managing zones

Each zone card has Show on map, Rename and a ✕ to delete. To reshape one, use Arrange mode on the map and drag its points.

The spot marker system

Beds and parasols are interchangeable, so the fixed thing is the spot on the concrete, not the furniture. Fix a printed marker to the ground at each spot — never onto a bed, because beds get stacked and locked at night.

Printing markers

Print spot markers opens a sheet grouped by zone, one QR per spot with its code and type. Print, then mount them on the ground.

Morning checklist

Each morning tick spots off as your team puts furniture out. Mark all placed does a whole zone at once, Clear resets it. The counter resets itself every new day.

Layouts

Everything auto-saves as you work. Layouts are named snapshots — save one for high season, another for a quiet week, and jump between them. Export and Import move a layout between devices as a file.

Today at a glance

Check-ins, peak occupancy, revenue and how many are in use right now, all updating live as you work.

Closing the day

Files today’s numbers into the history and puts every bed and parasol back to free, ready for tomorrow. It asks first, and it also clears the morning checklist.

Prices

Set your currency, the sun bed price and the parasol price. Takings are worked out per item, so a bed and a parasol on the same spot both count.

Online sync

Optional. Give it your own server address and every change plus each closed day is posted there as JSON. Test checks the address, Sync now pushes immediately. Without it everything still works offline.

History & export

The last fourteen days as two charts, with a table view underneath. Export day history saves the whole record as a CSV you can open in Excel.

Opening the scanner

From the rail, the card on Overview, or Scan inside a bed card. Your browser will ask for camera permission the first time.

How scanning works

Point the camera at a spot marker. The moment it reads, that spot changes status and the dashboard updates on every open device.

Scan modes

Check in only occupies, Set free only frees, Toggle flips whichever state the spot is in. Pick the mode that matches the round you are doing.

Typing a code

If a marker is scuffed or the light is bad, type the code by hand and press Go. Same result as scanning.

Feedback

A green flash and a rising chime mean checked in; a falling one means set free; a red flash and a buzz mean the code is not on this beach. Phones vibrate too.

Camera controls

Flip camera swaps front and back, and the torch button turns the light on where the hardware allows it. The side panel logs every scan of the session.
